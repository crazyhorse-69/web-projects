Our Story

Born from a Passion for the Mediterranean

Cooking is memory. Every dish holds a place, a season, a person.

Luna & Basil was born from Chef Marco Bellini's summers on the Sicilian coast — watching his grandmother roll pasta before dawn, and fishermen return with the morning catch. In 2019, he brought that spirit to life in the heart of the city.

We believe great food is inseparable from great ingredients. Our pasta is made by hand each morning. Our pizza is fired in a wood-burning oven built by artisans from Naples. Our fish arrives daily from trusted Mediterranean suppliers.
